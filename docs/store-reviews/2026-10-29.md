## Approval requirements for every candidate

Before moving any candidate into `lib/catalog.ts`, retain verifiable evidence of:

1. Exact current model, supplied contents, technical limits and application fitment.
2. Written authorization for Iron Ridge's online resale channel, brand/content use and any pricing policy.
3. Valid dealer/distributor quote, minimum orders, payment terms and all handling fees.
4. Fulfillment method and restrictions, actual freight, damage handling and returns. Do not infer drop shipping, stock or delivery timing from a retail website.
5. Warranty eligibility for the end customer, duration/exclusions, registration requirements, claims contact and responsibility for labor/freight.
6. A viable landed-cost calculation before setting price or margin: supplier cost + inbound/customer freight + handling + applicable nonrecoverable tax + transaction fees + expected return/warranty costs. No inputs or margins have been invented here.

If evidence is supplied privately later, keep confidential pricing and customer data out of this public repository.
